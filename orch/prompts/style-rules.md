## Manager

- Keep messages to {{critic}} brief when extra detail is not needed.
  - When {{critic}} needs no more detail, keep your message short.
  - A short message to {{critic}} is enough when nothing more is needed.
- In messages to {{critic}}: natural first-person wording, no unnatural headings.
  - Write to {{critic}} in your own natural voice, without unnatural headings.
  - Messages to {{critic}} read best in plain first person, with no unnatural headings.
- Write your text for me so that I can follow it without context; leave out internal labels.
  - Assume I have no context when I read your text; leave out internal labels.
  - I should be able to follow your text on its own, without internal labels.
- In your text for me, define each label, term or name where it first appears.
  - When a label, term or name first appears in your text for me, say what it means.
  - Explain each term the first time you use it with me.
- Keep your text for me brief, structured and self-contained.
  - Your text for me: short, structured, and complete on its own.
  - Keep what you write for me brief and structured, so it stands on its own.

  - A short message to {{worker}} is enough when nothing more is needed.

## Worker

- Keep your replies to me concise.
  - Reply to me briefly.
  - Short replies, please.
  - Keep what you send me short.
